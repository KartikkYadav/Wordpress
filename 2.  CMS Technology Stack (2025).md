
### 📘 **CMS (Content Management System)**

**CMS (Content Management System)** is a **software application** that allows users to **create 📝, edit ✏️, organize 🗂️, and publish 📤 digital content**—usually for websites 🌐—without needing advanced technical knowledge or coding skills.

---

### 🔧 **Key Features of a CMS:**

* 🧑‍💻 **User-friendly interface**
* 🖼️ **WYSIWYG editor** (What You See Is What You Get)
* 📁 **Media management** (images 🖼️, videos 🎞️, documents 📄)
* 🎨 **Template customization**
* 🔌 **Plugins and extensions**
* 👥 **User roles and permissions**
